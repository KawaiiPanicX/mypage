Privacy Policy for My Player

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

Welcome to My Player. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

My Player is designed primarily as a local media player. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data or media files to external servers.

  • Local Media Files: The app accesses video and audio files stored on your device solely to display and play them within the application.
  • Device & Usage Data: Non-personally identifiable information (e.g., app crash reports, device model, and OS version) may be processed locally or via Google Play Services to ensure performance stability.

2. App Permissions

To provide video playback functionality and advanced features, My Player requires certain permissions:

  • Storage / Media Library Access (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`): Required to scan, list, and play video files stored locally on your device.
  • Picture-in-Picture & Overlay Permission: Optional permission to enable background playback or pop-up video windows while using other apps.
  • System Settings Access: Used exclusively to adjust screen brightness and audio volume via gestures while playing videos.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to third parties. If third-party tools (such as Google Play Services or Firebase Analytics) are integrated for performance monitoring, their data collection is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy

4. Data Security

Since My Player processes all media locally on your device, your videos and media content never leave your phone. We implement standard security practices within the app to ensure system integrity.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate action.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
